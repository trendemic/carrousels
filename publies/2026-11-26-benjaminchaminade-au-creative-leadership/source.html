Creative leadership

Creativity needs structure

What creative leadership is, what it isn't, and what it depends on.

Common ground

Every executive wants more ideas.

Nobody in your leadership team will argue with that.

Fewer set up the conditions for ideas to appear. That's where creative leadership starts.

2/9
A working definition

A leader who goes beyond managing.

They inspire, they facilitate and they grow a culture of imagination, boldness and collaboration.

3/9
What it is

Six traits that define it

Time set aside to innovate, beyond today's problems

Listening, and openness to other disciplines

Flexibility when the ground shifts

Calculated risks, with failure treated as learning

A long view, beyond quick results

Open collaboration across skills

4/9
What it isn't

Strict control and rigid hierarchy

Improvisation with no structure

Reserved for artistic jobs

A free pass on concrete, measurable results

5/9
My view

Finance needs creative leaders too.

Creativity reaches well beyond creative industries.

When innovation is global, creativity decides which companies keep up.

6/9
Hard to line up at once

Leaders willing to break top‑down habits and hear ideas without judging them

A culture open to failure that democratises innovation

People hungry for challenges rather than docile

Four styles, four uses

Directive
fast starts, structured tasks

Delegative
autonomy for creative projects

Participative
engagement and shared ideas

Transformational
vision for change

8/9
Bottom line

Creative leadership is built on purpose.

Through experimentation, curiosity and collaboration.
