Regenerative management

Your manager is a gardener

What regenerative management asks of a leadership team.

Burnout is climbing and fewer people want to manage.

I read that distress as a symptom of a management model at its limits.

My definition

Preparing managers and teams for today's challenges

By valuing their past experience, acknowledging their present concerns and preparing them for their future.

Where it starts

What drains people for no value?

Pointless meetings, absurd reporting and endless sign-offs.

Remove those first.

5/9
Two managers

One extracts, one cultivates.

The extractive manager measures what he has obtained.

The regenerative one asks whether each person is more capable and better prepared than a year ago.

6/9
Next to regenerative leadership

An umbrella and a pair of boots

Regenerative leadership is the theoretical umbrella.

Regenerative management is pulling on your boots and starting to dig.
