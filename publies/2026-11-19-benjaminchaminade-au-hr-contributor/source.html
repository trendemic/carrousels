The finish line became the starting line

Why HR business partner is giving way to business contributor.

The title every People function wanted

HR business partner was the highest compliment.

A seat at the leadership table. A voice in strategy. Proof that HR had moved past administration.

The catch… strategy still comes from elsewhere, and HR helps execute it.

What moves when HR contributes

5/9
Where agentic AI lands

AI absorbs the execution work. Judgement stays.

Reading the culture. Naming the incoherences. Deciding what deserves to be measured.

Policy questions, job ads and analytics no longer need an experienced HR professional.

Experience, capability, coherence: the words are there. AI pilots and skills frameworks too.

Then an emergency lands on the desk…

and the function runs like a service provider again.
