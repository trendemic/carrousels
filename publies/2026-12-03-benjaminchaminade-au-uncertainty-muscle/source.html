Leadership

Uncertainty is a muscle

A capability you can train.

Everyone agrees uncertainty is here to stay.

Leaders still have to decide, with less information and on a shorter clock.

2/9
Trap one

Dismiss the signal

Kenneth Olsen, then head of Digital Equipment Corporation, saw no reason for anyone to own a home computer.

The error felt reasonable at the time.

3/9
Trap two

Dramatise it

A leader convinced nothing can be known drops analytical rigour and runs on instinct.

It feels decisive and performs badly.

4/9
Principle one

Anchor yourself in what stays fixed

A business decision predicts the best outcome. A principled decision holds whatever the outcome.

When the pandemic wiped out Airbnb's forecast, Brian Chesky set out principles instead.

5/9
Principle two

Build in order to learn

A meeting about an idea produces opinions. A meeting about an object produces evidence.

IDEO's working rule: never come to a meeting without a prototype.

6/9
Principle three

Row in the fog

The frame you put on ambiguity, out loud and in front of your team, sets how the room thinks for the rest of the meeting.

7/9
Before you fund anything

Find where the waiting sits

1

Same name behind every stalled decision: coach that person

2

Different names, same behaviour: train the team

3

Waiting on a rule: change the rule

8/9
Over to you

Your last stalled decision: who was everyone waiting for?
