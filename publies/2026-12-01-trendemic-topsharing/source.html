Two people, one management post

How Topsharing works, and where it falls short.

trendemicA world of ideas for better leadership

A leadership role usually means one person.

In Switzerland, Topsharing splits it between two, with shared powers and joint answerability for results.

trendemicA world of ideas for better leadership2/9
Where it comes from

A term introduced by Julia K. Kuark in 2002

Job sharing itself started in the United States in the 1970s.

Some Swiss sources call the leadership version Co-Leitung, or co-leadership.

trendemicA world of ideas for better leadership3/9
How it works

Four mechanics

The pair applies together for the post

Each partner signs an individual contract

Combined workload can exceed 100%

Decisions under a four-eyes principle

Who uses it

SBB funds each co-led post at 120%.

At health insurer CSS, two managers share a department head role at 60% each.

A 2023 survey by FHNW

28% of 1,064 Swiss organisations

had job sharing or Topsharing, in a survey commissioned by Verein PTO.

Among those, 35% had Topsharing.

Limits

Read these before you copy it

In 44% of organisations with job sharing, every pair was two women

Pairs run at 110% to 140% of one post

Small samples, and a survey commissioned by a promoter
